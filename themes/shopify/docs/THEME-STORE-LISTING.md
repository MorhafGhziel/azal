## Submission notes
- **Theme submission contact:** simastudio7@gmail.com. Allowlist theme-submissions@shopify.com and noreply@shopify.com.
- **Demo store password:** the storefront password from the demo store's Online Store › Preferences (you enter it in the form).
- **Testing instructions:**
  1. Open the home page and scroll slowly: the sky, turning bottle, Origins story, notes and collection slider are all scroll-driven. The mid-scroll "Begin the journey" button jumps to the story.
  2. Menu › Shop opens The fragrances collection with tag tabs, filters and sorting.
  3. Open Rose & Oud: the product background, character line, notes, story and how to wear it all come from product metafields (custom.*).
  4. Add to cart opens the cart drawer; cart type, notes and gift wrapping are in Theme settings › Cart.
  5. Switch the store language to Arabic to review right-to-left.
  6. Theme settings › Animations turns every motion effect off; reduced-motion devices get a still version.
- **Review notes:**
  AZAL is for small fragrance, beauty and lifestyle brands with a handful of signature products who sell on story more than on catalog size. The scroll-driven home page gives them a brand film without video production, and product metafields let each product carry its own color, notes and story, which raises time on page and gives merchants a premium look with no apps. The theme is built on Skeleton, uses only the Shopify font library, and ships English and Arabic with full right-to-left support.
